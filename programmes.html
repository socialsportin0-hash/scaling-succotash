What We Run

Five interconnected strands that build skills, confidence and stronger communities.

Sports

Weekly football, basketball and multi-sport sessions building skills, teamwork and confidence for all ages.

Mentoring

One-to-one and group mentoring pairing young people with trained mentors who guide the next generation.

Mental Health

Wellbeing workshops and safe spaces supporting mind and mental health alongside physical activity.

Digital Innovation

Sportin PRO AI and digital tools that bring performance tracking and coaching insight to every session.

Community

Volunteer-led events and partnerships that bring neighbourhoods together, stronger together.
